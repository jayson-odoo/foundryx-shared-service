## Steps
1. Sidebar: AutoCount > Companies > SRT > Entities tab. On the Stock balance row open Actions > Configure source (a click on the row itself does nothing), then open the Schedule tab.
2. Read the "Off hours" card (beside Incremental, Reconcile and Delete guard).
3. Click Edit.
4. Turn the Off hours switch On.
5. Set From = 22:00 and To = 22:00.
6. Click Save task.
7. Change To to 07:00.
8. Set From = NOW and To = END, then click Save task.
9. Read the Incremental card's "Next" badge and the Reconcile card's "Next" badge.
10. Reload the page and open the Schedule tab again.
11. Click Edit, set From = START-1 and To = 03:00, click Save task.
12. Read the Incremental card's "Next" badge.
13. Click Edit, set From = 01:00 and To = 02:00, click Save task.
14. Read the Incremental card's "Next" badge.
15. Click Edit, turn the Off hours switch Off, click Save task.
16. Read the Incremental card's "Next" badge.
17. Narrow the browser window to phone width (about 375 px) and scroll to the Off hours card.

## Expected
- Step 2: the card shows the switch "Off", From 22:00, To 07:00, and "MYT, every day". The From/To inputs are locked while the switch is Off.
- Step 4: From/To unlock.
- Step 5: red text under To: "The off hours must end at a different time than they start." (shown before saving).
- Step 6: the save is refused and the same red text stays under To.
- Step 7: the red text under To disappears.
- Step 8: toast "Task saved."
- Step 9: Incremental "Next" = today, END (the window end, not NOW + 15 min). Reconcile still at 02:00 UTC (Next 10 Nov, 10:00): the window never moves the reconcile.
- Step 10: the card still reads On, From NOW, To END. Incremental "Next" is still today, END.
- Step 12: Incremental "Next" = tomorrow, 03:00 (the window crosses midnight and NOW is inside it).
- Step 14: Incremental "Next" = about NOW + 15 min (NOW is outside a 01:00-02:00 window).
- Step 16: Incremental "Next" = about NOW + 15 min, and the card's switch reads Off.
- Step 17: the cards stack in one column, the Off hours card is fully visible, and there is no sideways scrolling.
