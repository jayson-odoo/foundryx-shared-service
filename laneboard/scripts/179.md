# Hand test - PR #179 SS-CHANNEL-SKIP (migrate now with the channel skipped, connect the channel later)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- No schema change, no data migration. Nothing to run beyond the normal boot.
- Sign in as a tenant Admin (holds `omnichannel_migration.manage` and the channel permissions).
- A respond.io connection under Settings > Integrations that reads a SMALL respond.io space with at least one WhatsApp channel and a few contacts with messages. respond.io is only read, never written.
- Create a NEW empty workspace for this test (Omnichannel > Settings > Workspaces > New, name `Skip test <today>`). Do NOT use MyFuji Platform: this test connects a channel to the workspace.
- The test copy must have NO real Meta app env (`NEXT_PUBLIC_META_APP_ID` unset). The Connect a channel wizard then uses its simulated sign-up and no real WhatsApp number is touched.

## Steps
1. Sidebar: Omnichannel > Settings > Migration, click "New migration".
2. Method: "respond.io Developer API". respond.io connection: pick the test connection.
3. Target workspace: pick `Skip test <today>`.
4. Look at the Channels card.
5. Leave every channel row on "Skip for now". Click "Run dry run", wait until "Start migration" is enabled, click "Start migration".
6. Wait for the status "Done". Note the Counts report rows Contacts, Channel identities, Messages.
7. Sidebar: Omnichannel > Contacts, open a migrated contact and send "test" in its conversation.
8. Back on the migration setup (Omnichannel > Settings > Migration > New migration, same connection and workspace), click "Connect channel" on a channel row.
9. In the new tab, connect a WhatsApp channel to `Skip test <today>` (Channel type WhatsApp, Attach to workspace `Skip test <today>`, Continue, finish the simulated popup). Close the tab.
10. Back in the migration tab, change Target workspace to another workspace and then back to `Skip test <today>` (this reloads the channel list).
11. On the WhatsApp row pick the channel you just connected. Click "Run dry run", wait, click "Start migration", wait for "Done".
12. Open the same contact as step 7 in Omnichannel > Contacts and look at the conversation.
13. Run the migration a third time exactly like step 11 (same mapping).
14. Resize the browser to 375 px wide on the New migration page (steps 2-4), then back to 1280 px.

## Expected
- Step 4: each WhatsApp row shows its name, `whatsapp`, then "No channel connected" and an orange dashed "Connect channel" link. The picker reads "Skip for now". The Notice under the connection reads `No connected channel in the target workspace matches source channel "<name>" (whatsapp). Its contacts and history import without a channel.`
- Step 5: no error. Save/dry run/start are all allowed with every channel skipped.
- Step 6: Contacts "Would create" = the number of respond.io contacts; Channel identities all 0; Messages "Would create" = the message count; Errors 0.
- Step 7: the send is refused with "No active channel is connected to this workspace." (no crash, no message stuck as sending).
- Step 8: a NEW browser tab opens on the Channels page; the migration tab keeps everything you picked.
- Step 11: Channel identities "Would create" = number of contacts on that channel; Messages "Would create" 0, "Would update" = the messages from that respond.io channel, Errors 0.
- Step 12: the history is still there exactly once (no duplicates) and a new reply goes out on the connected channel.
- Step 13: Messages "Would update" 0 and "Would create" 0; Channel identities "Would create" 0; nothing duplicated.
- Step 14: at 375 px the Channels row stacks (name, "No channel connected  Connect channel", then the picker full width) with no sideways page scroll; at 1280 px it is side by side as in step 4.
