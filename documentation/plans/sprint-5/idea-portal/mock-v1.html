IDEA-0014

Dealer stock check from the chatbot

In progress Submitted by Aiman·2 Oct 2026 Your idea
Business problem
Dealers phone the HQ sales desk to ask if a model and colour is in stock. The desk answers 40 calls a day and the dealer waits for a callback.
Desired outcome
A dealer asks the WhatsApp bot "is the Sorento 2.2D in white available" and gets the stock count by branch straight away.
Use cases
  • Customer at the showroom asks for a colour that is not on the floor.
  • Dealer plans a weekend promotion and needs to know what can be delivered.
Current workaround
Call or WhatsApp the HQ desk and wait, usually 1 to 3 hours.
