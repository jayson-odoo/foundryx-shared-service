## Journey

A tenant admin opens Users from the sidebar on a laptop or a phone. The header and sidebar are
translucent materials over the content; the list header stays put while they scroll; every
status is a round tinted pill with a dot. They press a button and it answers on pointer-down.
They tap anywhere on a row and the record opens, carrying the list's page, sort, filters and
search in the URL. The record page shows the title and crumbs on the left and ONE Back button on
the right; the record card shows the identity, then a prev/next pager, a gear holding the
secondary actions with Delete red and last, and one primary button. Every dialog, sheet, menu
and popover opens on the same critically damped spring, from its trigger, and can be re-opened
mid-close without a jump. When they delete, no dialog asks; the button becomes a countdown they
can cancel, and the server commits when the window lapses even if they closed the tab. When they
press Back, the list lands on the row they left, highlighted. When they change page, the rows
stay on screen dimmed until the next page arrives. Someone who asked their OS for less motion
sees the same interface with cross-fades instead of travel. Nothing looks different from
Sorento except the orange and the Poppins headings.
